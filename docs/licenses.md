## Earth and Boundaries

- `earth-surface.jpg` and `earth-clouds.png` come from the Three.js planets texture collection; the surface derives from NASA Blue Marble.
- `countries-110m.geojson` comes from Natural Earth 1:110m country boundaries and is public domain.

Every new asset must document its author, original URL, license, and use.
